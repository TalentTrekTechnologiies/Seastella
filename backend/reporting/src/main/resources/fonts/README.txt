Fonts for generated documents (SBMC certificate, QM-F11 pre-survey form).

They stand in for the fonts the client's templates were made with, at the same
widths, so text lands where the template expects it:

  LiberationSerif-Regular/Bold  -> Times New Roman   (SIL OFL 1.1, LICENSE-Liberation.txt)
  Caladea-Bold                  -> Cambria Bold      (SIL OFL 1.1, OFL-Caladea.txt)
  Carlito-Regular/Bold          -> Calibri           (SIL OFL 1.1, OFL-Carlito.txt)

Microsoft's own fonts cannot be shipped on the server; these may be.
