SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS & SYSTEM FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION
Maritime Navigation Equipment Asset & Service Management Platform

1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE
This document defines the functional, technical, operational, security, workflow, and access-control requirements for the proposed Maritime Navigation Equipment Asset & Service Management Platform for SeaStella.
The document consolidates:
Requirements and Use Cases provided by SeaStella
Proposed functional modules by Talent Trek Technologies
User roles and access control
Equipment and maintenance workflows
Service and vendor management
Spare and procurement workflows
Financial approval and payment tracking
Automated alerts and deadline management
Security and audit requirements
Four-week MVP implementation plan
Acceptance criteria and pending client confirmations

2. REQUIREMENT CLASSIFICATION
To avoid confusion between confirmed client requirements and implementation recommendations, requirements are classified as follows.
CLIENT – CONFIRMED

PROPOSED – TALENT TREK

TBD – CLIENT CONFIRMATION

Platform administration

6. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL
The application must use Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
Access must be enforced at the backend/API level and must not depend only on hiding buttons or screens in the frontend.
Role | Organization | Vessel | Equipment | Service | Finance | Administration
Platform Admin | Full | Full | Full | Full | Global | Full
Technical Head | Assigned | Assigned Fleet | View/Manage as permitted | View/Review | TBD | No
Ship Manager | Assigned | Assigned | Manage | Manage/Approve | TBD | No
Captain | Assigned | Assigned Vessel | View/Update | Raise | No | No
Chief Engineer | Assigned | Assigned Vessel | Manage | Raise/Manage | No | No
Service Coordinator | Assigned Scope | Assigned Scope | Related | Manage | TBD | No
Service Provider | Restricted | Related | Related Job | Assigned Jobs | No | No

14. MAINTENANCE COLOUR STATUS
The platform will use dynamic colour indicators to make approaching deadlines immediately visible.
Remaining Time | Status | Colour
More than 15 days | Normal | 🟢 Green
10–15 days | Approaching | 🟡 Yellow
1–9 days | Urgent | 🟠 Orange
Due Today | Due | 🔴 Red
Past Due Date | Overdue | 🔴 Red
Recommended Implementation
The threshold values should be configurable from the Administration module rather than hard-coded.
This allows SeaStella to change the warning period in the future without requiring application redevelopment.
TBD – Client Confirmation
SeaStella should confirm:
Exact warning thresholds

15. MAINTENANCE ALERTS
When an equipment deadline approaches, the system should automatically generate notifications.
Possible Notification Channels
In-App Notifications
Email Notifications
Example
Equipment: Radar System
Service Due: 15 September
If the current date reaches the configured warning period:
Status → Yellow
As the deadline becomes closer:
Status → Orange
After the deadline:
Status → Red / Overdue
The system should notify the appropriate users based on configured rules.
